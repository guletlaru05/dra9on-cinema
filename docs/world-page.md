The owner confirmed Min Seo-a is the sole lead. Iseo, Yeonhwa, Yuna and the
other seven supporting roles appear in a separate supporting section.
Han is a male 707 assault operator. Kim handles medical support and communications.

Twelve episode cards cover S1 EP.1–6 and S2 EP.1–6. The 20 additional assets
are recorded in `site-assets/assets/world/manifest.json`, with actual output

instead of enlarging the small supplied screenshot. The owner requested that the extra action still be removed; only the selected portrait remains. No new images were
generated for this update.

The portrait relationship explorer uses native disclosure controls, grouped by
707, Joseon, Mokpojin and the black bell. Only established roles, family ties,
companionship and visible opposition appear. No link reveals the elder shaman's
hidden connection, allegiance, a survival status or the outcome of a rescue.
Iseo and Yeonhwa are not described as siblings or romantic partners.

Spoiler review covers copy and imagery. Episode 3 does not reveal the nature of
the missing operators; Episode 5 does not describe the shamans' past; the finales
do not state results. Portraits exclude infection/transformation imagery. Season 3
is not included in this world page. All four languages use the same restricted scope.
