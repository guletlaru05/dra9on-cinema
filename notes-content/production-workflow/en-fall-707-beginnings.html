AI FILMMAKING · SEASON 1 · AFTERWORD

From early experiments to FALL 707 Season 1

I began with something I loved, gave up for a time after receiving a lot of criticism, then returned thanks to the people waiting for the next episode. This is the first FALL 707 production note.

DRA9ON CINEMA / 2026.09.27

01

Hello, this is DRA9ON CINEMA!

The plan: keep it short. Very short.

It started with a love of military films.

Today, I want to share how FALL 707 began. I did not start with a grand universe in mind. I simply loved military films and wanted to try making a short one.

02

Modern tactics. An unfamiliar era.

…It was not just a change of scenery.

But… what if they landed in Joseon?

The initial idea was this: what if a modern special forces soldier travelled back to Joseon, fought its soldiers and tried to escape? That became the first film, about two minutes long. This was the starting point of the early experiments; in the reboot, the main enemies became a shaman villain and zombies.

03

One more episode.

I meant to make just one. Somehow, there were four.

Four early experiments, between encouragement and harsh comments.

Some people liked the story. Others left pointed criticism or harsh comments. Encouragement kept me going through four early episodes, each around one to two minutes. These were experiments made before the official Season 1.

04

For a time, I gave up.

Wait. Can I really keep going like this?

Honestly, I gave up for a time.

In the early experiments, I did not get the unit patches or the Joseon-period details right. I received a lot of criticism. I was still new to AI drama and did not know how to maintain character consistency. Eventually, I gave up making FALL 707 for a time.

ACTUAL FRAMES / EARLY EXPERIMENTS

Why the early experiments drew criticism: the actual frames.

In the early experiments, I did not get either the unit patches or the Joseon-period details right. I received a lot of criticism and gave up making FALL 707 for a time. I was still learning AI drama and character consistency. These are the images that remained in those films.

At 00:10 and 00:21.2 in the same pilot, the left-arm patches have different emblems and lettering.

Beyond the face: the pilot and second experiment show why the helmet, vest and clothing also need a shared reference.

These are actual frames from the original films. The detail images crop the patch area.

05

I liked FALL 707. Are you making any more?

Those words… sent me back to the desk.

The words that brought me back.

Then I heard this more than once. Someone was waiting for the story I had given up for a time. Those words gave me the energy to try again.

06

FALL 707: REBOOT

Next on the short-film to-do list: rebuild an entire world.

Instead of the next episode, a new beginning.

I rebuilt the character sheets and reworked the world and story from the ground up. Rather than simply continuing those early experiments, I started over. With a shaman villain and zombies as the main enemies, the story shifted toward modern tactics confronting unfamiliar occult threats. That rebuild became the official Season 1 of FALL 707: REBOOT, a military fantasy occult thriller. What began as a short video had become a world of my own that I wanted to keep exploring.

Min is a captain in the Republic of Korea Army’s 707th Special Mission Group. Season 1 presented her in a black uniform and ponytail, with her human side visible. The later facial injury is not included in this afterword’s Season 1 illustrations.
